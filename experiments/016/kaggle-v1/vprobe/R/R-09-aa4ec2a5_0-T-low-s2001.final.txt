To solve the ARC puzzle, we need to analyze the transformation rules from the provided demos.

**Analysis of Demos:**

1.  **Identify Components:** The input grids contain a background of `4`s and shapes formed by `1`s. We identify connected components of `1`s using 4-way connectivity (up, down, left, right).
2.  **Determine Shape Properties:** For each component of `1`s, we check if it encloses any "holes". A hole is a region of `4`s that is completely surrounded by `1`s (and not connected to the outer background of `4`s).
3.  **Transformation Rules:**
    *   **Border:** For every component, a border of `2`s is drawn. The border consists of all `4` cells that are 4-adjacent to any cell in the component (either a `1` or a filled hole `6`).
    *   **Filling:**
        *   If the component has **no holes**, the `1`s remain `1`.
        *   If the component **has holes**, the `1`s change to `8`, and the hole cells (enclosed `4`s) change to `6`.
    *   **Background:** All other `4`s remain `4`.
